#### Design Choices & Why

- **Colors:**
  - **Emerald & Teal (`#10b981`, `#0d9488`)**: Selected based on behavioral psychology to evoke calm, clarity, and positive reinforcement rather than aggressive, anxiety-inducing red penalties.
  - **Slate Scales (Light `#f8fafc` / Dark `#020617`)**: Engineered for WCAG 2.1 AA high-contrast readability, reducing ocular fatigue during morning and bedtime ritual reviews.
  - **Vibrant Accent Tones**: Warm amber for active streaks and indigo for weekly consistency to establish instant visual hierarchy without visual clutter.

- **Fonts:**
  - **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Logo)**: A geometric sans-serif that communicates modern refinement, purposeful intent, and approachable warmth.
  - **Inter (Body & Controls)**: Renowned for exceptional legibility at small sizes, tall x-height, and clean numerical tabular figures for streak tracking.

- **Spacing & Layout:**
  - **8pt Spacing Grid**: Strict rhythm (`p-4`, `p-6`, `gap-3`, `gap-4`) creating ample breathing space between interactive elements to prevent cognitive overload.
  - **44px Minimum Touch Targets**: Ensures comfortable ergonomics across mobile touchscreens and keyboard focus rings.
